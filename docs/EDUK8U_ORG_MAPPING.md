# EDUK8U Group — org chart mapping

How the "EDUK8U® Group - Org Chart" approved by MD on 16 October 2024 was
transcribed into HR Nexus, what is certain, and what Dr. Roy needs to confirm.

This document and the environment it describes are a **review copy**. Nothing
here has been sent to, or taken from, any EDUK8U production system.

## How the hierarchy was read

The reporting lines were read from the connector elements inside the original
PowerPoint. Each connector there records the two shapes it joins, so the lines
are data rather than something inferred from the order the text happens to sit
in. The exported PNG was then used to check the arrowheads and the grouping
brackets, which the connector data alone does not settle.

That matters because the text of the chart, read on its own, does not say who
reports to whom at all. Anything derived only from reading order would have been
a guess.

## What was deliberately not created

The source names **no people**. It is a chart of positions. So:

- Every entry is loaded as an unfilled **position** or as an **external** party,
  and none is loaded as an employed person. The org chart marks both in words,
  not only by styling.
- No names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, start dates,
  salaries or identity-card numbers were created for any of them. Where the
  source has nothing, the field is empty.
- No attendance, leave, payroll, goals, reviews or recognition exist in this
  environment. Inventing them would show Dr. Roy figures about his own company
  that nobody measured.

## Questions for Dr. Roy

Six reporting lines cannot be settled from the chart. The environment shows each
of them as recorded-but-unconfirmed rather than choosing quietly.

1. **Does the Group Chief Marketing Officer & Co-Founder report to the Group CEO
   & CHRO?** The chart draws no line between them. The two marketing executives
   point up into the CMO, but nothing continues upward. The chart therefore has
   two tops, and HR Nexus shows two separate branches. If the CMO does report to
   the Group CEO, one change joins them.

2. **Who does the Admin Intern (Tracking, Support, Docs) report to?** It hangs
   off the bracket down the left of the BD column, which joins all four boxes
   there, but unlike the three BD executives it has no arrow of its own up to
   the Group CEO. It is currently placed under *1. Snr BD Exec Recruitment*.

3. **Who do the two Marketing GD Interns report to?** The chart draws two
   arrows, one into each marketing executive. An employee record in HR Nexus
   holds one manager, so only the line to *1. Marketing Exec.* is recorded and
   the second is lost. If the split is real, it needs to be stated in words.

4. **Who do the two Tech Interns report to?** Their line joins the spine that
   runs past all three developers, which reads as "the developer group" rather
   than any one of them. Currently placed under *1. Tech Developer*.

5. **Do the two Liaison Officers (1: 36) really report to one another?** The
   chart draws a line between them, but unlike the line above it that line is
   attached to neither box in the source file. Two posts with the same title and
   the same ratio are at least as likely to be peers who both report to the
   Welfare Liaison. Recorded as drawn and shown as unconfirmed; see "The two
   Liaison Officers" below.

6. **Who does the "CEO Special Officer" report to?** Ameer has confirmed the
   post itself: it is **one position**, an **individual contributor**, and it
   **leads nobody**. "Individual Contributor" is the description of that post,
   not a second post, so no card was created for it - it is carried as the
   post's own job title. What remains open is only the reporting line: the words
   sit at the top right as free text, outside any box and with no connector at
   all, so the line to the Group CEO is shown as unconfirmed.

Two further points are not ambiguities but are worth confirming:

- **"TBH"** appears on three boxes (Welfare Liaison, and both Liaison Officers).
  Its meaning was not established, so it has been left exactly as written rather
  than expanded into a guess.
- **Boxes standing for more than one person** — "Marketing: GD Intern KL X 2"
  and "2 X Tech Intern" — are kept as one entry each, as the chart draws them.
  Say the word and each becomes two positions.

## Confidence

- **Confirmed** — the PowerPoint binds a connector to both boxes and the arrow
  points from the position to the manager shown.
- **Likely grouping** — the box reaches its manager through an elbow or spine
  shared with its peers. The manager is not in doubt; only the individual line
  is drawn through a junction.
- **Ambiguous** — the chart does not settle it. Each is a numbered question
  above.

### Connectors 65 and 77: drawn, but deliberately not stored

Two lines in the source cannot become reporting lines without saying something
false, so neither was stored:

- **65** would make *3. Snr BD Exec Recruitment* report to *2. Snr BD Exec
  Recruitment*, while connector **63** simultaneously routes the same box to the
  Group CEO. A box cannot report in two places. Both 64 and 65 run down the left
  edges of the BD column while the reporting arrows leave from the right, which
  is what a **grouping bracket** looks like, not a chain of command. That is also
  why 64 (the Admin Intern) is recorded as ambiguous rather than as a fact.
- **77** is the second of two lines out of the GD Intern box. An employee record
  holds one manager, so the line to *2. Marketing Exec* has nowhere to live.

### The two Liaison Officers

The chain is **retained as drawn, and marked unconfirmed**. The evidence:

- Connector **53**, from *Liaison Officer* to *Welfare Liaison & Client Service
  AU*, is **bound at both ends**. That line is a fact.
- Connector **59**, between the two *Liaison Officer* boxes, is **bound at
  neither end**. It is a vertical line at x = 11.14in running from y = 3.38 to
  y = 3.77, which lands in the gap between the first box's lower edge (3.42) and
  the second box's upper edge (3.79). Its position says "these two", but the file
  never attached it to either.

So the source **draws** the line and **does not confirm** it. Two posts sharing a
title and a (1: 36) ratio are at least as likely to be peers under the Welfare
Liaison, stacked for layout. Replacing the drawn line with a guessed one would
have been inventing; deleting it would have thrown away something the source does
show. It is kept, and the card says **"Reporting line unconfirmed"**.

### The Group CEO's ten direct reports

All ten are supported, but not equally, so the chart does not present them as one
uniform claim:

- **Five are bound connectors**: Company Accountant, 1. Snr BD Exec, 3. Tech
  Developer, Head of Compliance & Quality Management, Welfare Liaison & Client
  Service AU.
- **Four are inferred from shared routing** — 2. and 3. Snr BD Exec, and 1. and
  2. Tech Developer. This is not "they share a bracket, so they must report
  here". In each case the stub's own endpoint coordinates land on the elbow of a
  connector that *is* bound to the CEO: connectors 62 and 63 end on connector
  61's trunk, and connectors 69 and 70 end on connector 68's spine, whose bend
  sits at x = 7.10in, exactly where those stubs stop.
- **One is not supported by any connector**: *CEO Special Officer* is free text
  with no line at all, placed under the CEO because that is what its label says.
  It is marked unconfirmed.

## Where this lives in the code

The mapping above is generated from `backend/src/database/eduk8uData.ts`, which
is the single source of truth for the review environment. Correcting the chart
means editing that file and re-running the bootstrap, which is described in
[EDUK8U_REVIEW_SETUP.md](EDUK8U_REVIEW_SETUP.md). `backend/tests/eduk8u-org.test.ts`
holds the mapping to the connector list above, so an accidental change to a
reporting line fails a test rather than reaching Dr. Roy.

## How the chart is drawn

The chart is a reconstruction of the source drawing, not an automatic layout.
Every box is placed at the coordinates the PowerPoint gives it (`a:off` and
`a:ext` on each shape), scaled up so that a full-size HR Nexus card fits in the
space the author left for it, and the connectors are derived from those
positions. So the marketing column is on the left, business development beside
it, the Group CEO above the middle, then finance, technology, compliance and the
welfare desk rightwards - as on the source - and the external advisers sit below
the source's own dashed rule.

Two things are drawn that are not positions: the source's **SALES / BD Function**
reference box, which lists the four streams that function sells into, and its
**approval footer**. Both are stored as labelled notes with their own
coordinates, and neither can ever become an employee record.

The chart is shown at one fixed, readable scale and scrolled like any other
page - sideways within the chart, up and down with the page. It is not zoomed,
panned or scaled to fit, because a chart shrunk until it fits is a chart nobody
can read. The printed copy is the one exception: it is scaled once onto a single
A3 landscape sheet.
